TERMS

review the Terms first.

Here you’ll find how I handle projects, payments, and revisions. Reading this helps keep our collaboration smooth and clear.

1. Commission Process and Agreement

1.1 Brief and Agreement
  • The Client must provide a clear brief outlining their vision for the commission.
  • If the Client lacks a concrete concept, TowerJunkieArt will assist in developing one through consultation.
  • The brief may include a rough sketch with annotations explaining key elements.
  • The Client must specify required dimensions for the final artwork.
1.2 Work Process
  • Work begins only after payment is received.
  • TowerJunkieArt provides progress updates via email.
  • Feedback must be timely.
  • Adjustments made until commission is finalized.
1.3 Revisions
  • Minor revisions included at no extra cost.
  • Significant revisions incur $35 USD per revision.
  • PayPal fees apply.
1.4 Deadlines and Rush Orders
  • Client specifies deadlines during briefing.
  • Deadlines under 2 weeks may increase price.
  • Deadlines under 5 days not accepted.
1.5 Delivery and Rights
  • Completed work delivered after full payment.
  • Client receives full rights to use final design.
  • TowerJunkieArt may display work in portfolio.

2. Payment Terms

2.1 Payment Structure
  • First-time clients, commissions over $200 USD: 50% upfront, 50% on completion.
  • Returning clients may pay full upfront.
  • Commissions under $200 USD: full payment upfront.
2.2 Payment Method
  • All payments via PayPal.
  • PayPal fee included in invoice.
  • All prices in USD.

3. Contract and Agreement

  • Client receives formal contract after agreeing commission details.
  • Contract includes final price, project description, delivery and usage rights.
  • Contract must be signed before work begins.
