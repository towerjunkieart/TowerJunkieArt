ABOUT ME

A QUICK INTRO

I'm TowerJunkieArt, a visual artist and designer from Serbia, working independently since 2017.

I specialize in environment concept art, book/album covers and visual identities. I work alone and handle projects end-to-end, which allows for a consistent vision and attention to detail.

Over the years, I've collaborated with authors, musicians and creative teams, creating visuals that are carefully developed, meaningful, and built to last. I approach each project with focus and attention, exploring ideas fully before committing to a final design, drawing inspiration from literature, music, video games, and film.

SERVICES

Your dream, my art.

Behind every visual I create is a story. I dive into the essence of each project, exploring its mood, narrative, and purpose, and then translate that into designs that communicate, resonate, and inspire. From intricate concept art to bold branding, my goal is to turn ideas into experiences that leave a mark, because design should be more than decoration - it should speak.
